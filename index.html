// 03   Manifesto

No Filler.
No Trends.

Why we work the way we work — and what we won't compromise on.

Every game we ship has a distinct voice. We don't clone what's popular. We build the thing we want to play — and trust the right players will find it.
  • 01

    Identity Over Trend-Chasing

    No clones, no copycats. Every project has a point of view from the first line of code.

  • 02

    Players First, Always

    No predatory monetization. No dark patterns. Retention through quality, not friction.

  • 03

    Ship, Learn, Improve

    Tight loops. We release, watch, listen, iterate. The community is part of the team.

  • 04

    Independent, For Real

    No publishers. No investors. Decisions stay with the people making the games.
